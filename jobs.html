We Are Hiring

Event Styling Coordinator

Casual · 16–24 hours per week · Windsor, Melbourne

We're looking for an event stylist & coordinator to join SAID STUDIO.

Working closely with our creative director, you'll be involved across client projects from concept through to event day — helping develop ideas, coordinate the details and bring them to life on site.

It's a hands-on role for someone who sits comfortably between creative and organised. You might spend one day developing a styling presentation or sourcing for a project, and the next working with venues, suppliers and our team to deliver an event.

We're looking for someone with around 2–3 years' relevant industry experience, a strong visual eye and the confidence to take ownership and make things happen.
